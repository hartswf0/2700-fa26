P0

Premise

What kind of learning counts?

01Thought
Cf
Confusion is proof of thought
Confusion can mark the edge of understanding, not its absence.
open note

Visible confusion can be evidence that a student has reached the edge of what they understand. The instructional task is not simply to eliminate confusion, but to make students inspect, articulate, and work through it.

Seed: “Proof of thought” / “Confusion.”

03Primitive

The primitive before the prompt
Begin with operations small enough to understand end-to-end.

Begin with one visible operation, then variables, repetition, collections, data, and systems. Only after students can relate an instruction to an observable consequence should generative systems enter the loop.

06Aesthetic
As
Labs teach aesthetic structure
A computational operation should acquire expressive consequence.
open note

A loop is not only repetition; an array is not only storage; randomness is not only a function. Each lab should teach what a computational structure can become perceptually.

Not just workshops
The unit of instruction is agency gained, not feature mastery.
open note

A workshop teaches how to operate something. A course should also ask why the operation matters, what it makes possible, what choices it encodes, and what the student can make with it.

P6

Assessment

Make requirements legible; reserve judgment for distinction.

15Grade
90
90% of the grade should be objective
Students should know most requirements in advance.
open note

Completion, required behaviors, milestones, technical requirements, documentation, participation, and submission can carry most of the grade. This reduces the burden of repeatedly turning aesthetic judgment into numbers.
