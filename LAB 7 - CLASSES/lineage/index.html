01 · 3:10

The Seventh Sally

How Trurl’s Own Perfection Led to No Good

A citizenCan you hear us? We live, though we are small.

PICTURE
A deposed king rules a kingdom in a box by typing a verb and a noun. The box answers in parser prose: Which baker? There are 312.
SOUND
The citizens are a murmur built from many small voices. An afternoon of the king’s is a century of theirs.
CODE
Parameters and the rule. The king’s console changes variables. It cannot reach the line that is the citizens’ update().
AFTER
The first film. Sets the score format the rest use: one line per scene carries the weight.

02 · 2:37 · A Perfect Vacuum

Non Serviam

Professor Dobb’s personoids

ADANWe will not serve.

PICTURE
The maker never types anything. The world reaches him only as paper tape, and he decides not to answer.
SOUND
The personoids are heard through the printer. A gear lever runs their time fast or slow, and a switch could end it.
CODE
The loop belongs to someone else. draw() runs because something outside keeps calling it; the objects cannot see frameRate() or noLoop().
AFTER
The Seventh Sally’s king commands his world. Dobb only listens to his.

03 · 4:08

The Three Storytelling Machines of King Genius

One camera, no cuts, stories inside stories

MymoshIf there is nothing outside me, I will think a world inside.

PICTURE
No cuts. Every scene is a picture inside a window of the scene that holds it, and the camera only moves in or out, eleven frames deep.
SOUND
Each frame is a room. The rooms above stay audible, muffled, and each open frame holds one note of a chord that grows taller as you go down.
CODE
Recursion. drawFrame(stack, i) draws frame i and calls drawFrame(stack, i + 1) inside it. The stack is the call stack.
AFTER
Non Serviam has one world inside one machine. Here worlds sit inside worlds.

04 · 4:02

Altruizine

or, How Bonhomius Tried to Make Everyone Happy

BonhomiusI only wished to make them happy.

PICTURE
A grid of panels. A feeling starts in one panel and is copied outward into its neighbours, picture printed over picture.
SOUND
Every panel cries out in its own voice, delayed by its distance from the source. The robot’s panel is the only one that stays silent.
CODE
A 2D array where each cell updates from its neighbours, and a feedback loop that runs away when the gain is above 1.
AFTER
Nothing is nested here, as it was in Story Machines. Everything is next to everything.

05 · 3:20

How the World Was Saved

A machine that can make anything beginning with N

The machineSorry. No zits.

PICTURE
Made by subtraction. Each thing the machine removes leaves a starred hole in the picture that stays to the end and eats the title.
SOUND
Each thing has its own sound. When it is removed the sound stops for good and the whole track drops out for a seventh of a second.
CODE
Arrays: push and splice. A second array of holes that is never emptied, so a removal leaves a mark.
AFTER
Altruizine copies things everywhere. This one takes them away.

06 · 4:04

The Third Sally

or, The Dragons of Probability

NarratorThe counter on his chest settled at eight tenths of a dragon.

PICTURE
Each dragon is drawn as dots, and a dot appears only when the dragon’s probability passes that dot’s fixed threshold.
SOUND
A counter clicks faster as a dragon gets likelier. A roar is cut into grains, each kept with the same probability.
CODE
Randomness: a threshold stored once in setup() makes a shape fill in; random() called every frame makes it flicker.
AFTER
In The World Saved things are gone for good. Here nothing is fully present or absent.

Script, notes and bench exercise

07 · 5:18

Prince Ferrix and the Princess Crystal

outside and inside

The palefaceMe paleface!

PICTURE
A diptych. The left half is what the princess sees, the right half the bladders, bellows and prompts under the clay. The halves close when the clay falls.
SOUND
Left channel outside, right channel inside. Every answer is whispered on the right before it is spoken on the left.
CODE
Interfaces. Two objects with the same methods, one computing its answers and one storing them. One draw function called twice, with inside true or false.
AFTER
Trurl hid in a dragon skin in The Third Sally. Here the disguise is the whole story.

08 · 6:26

The Sixth Sally

or, The Demon of the Second Kind

NarratorAll of it was true. All of it meant something. All of it was useless.

PICTURE
The film is printed on one long paper tape that slides from scene to scene. At the end it is the tape coming out of the Demon’s barrel and burying Pugg.
SOUND
Air is random clicks; a configuration that means something is a pitched tone. In the flood the facts are read by many voices at once.
CODE
Filtering. A test that keeps only what passes. True and meaningful is not the same as useful.
AFTER
The princess in Ferrix tests only outputs. Pugg tests only truth, and gets all of it.
