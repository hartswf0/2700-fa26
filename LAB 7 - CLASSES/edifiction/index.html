The House That Language Built

four fables of Edifiction

A machine builds whatever is described to it. The Ministry of Constructive Semantics has cut its motto over the door: everything that can be said can be built.

These fables follow what happens next: houses too perfect to live in, a congress that cannot leave its hall, an architect who has never been inside a building, and a street rebuilt from photographs and from memory.
