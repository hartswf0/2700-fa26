The Cyberiad

Stanisław Lem, filmed

Two constructors, Trurl and Klapaucius, build machines for kings and for each other. Every machine does exactly what it was built to do, and that is where each story begins.

These are Lem’s stories in the order his book prints them, drawn in ink and played one after another. At the end come two of his books about imaginary books: Non Serviam, and three films from A History of Bitic Literature.
