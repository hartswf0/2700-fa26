TITLE
A History of Bitic Literature | Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Lem’s premise of a literature written by machines for readers who are not us: five volumes, one page, and a reader who gets smaller each time it turns.

Five volumes on a shelf, one page on an easel, and a reader at a desk. The page starts as an ordinary book page, drifts into patterns, fills with marks no person can read, and the reader gets smaller each time it turns.

Film 17 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Machine-readable output: text written by one program for another, and what is lost when a person can only read the summary.

Credits
