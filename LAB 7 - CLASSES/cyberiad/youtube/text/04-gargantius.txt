TITLE
The First Sally: The Trap of Gargantius | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Two kings preparing for war each hire a constructor to turn their army into a single mind. The armies are joined, and each becomes one thinker with better things to do than fight.

A graph drawn in ink. A soldier is a node and a connection is a line between two nodes.

Film 4 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
All the films: [playlist link]
The complete cut, 2 hours: [complete cut link]
In the browser: https://hartswf0.github.io/2700-fa26/LAB%207%20-%20CLASSES/cyberiad/

The programming idea: Objects that hold references to other objects. A network does things no single node was told to do, and a connection cannot be undone one node at a time.
