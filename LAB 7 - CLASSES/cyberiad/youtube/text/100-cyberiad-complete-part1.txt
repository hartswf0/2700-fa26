TITLE
The Cyberiad, filmed: Stanisław Lem’s robot stories in ink (Part 1 of 2)

DESCRIPTION
Part 1 of 2: three early stories and the first five sallies. Two constructors, Trurl and Klapaucius, build machines for kings and for each other. Every machine does exactly what it was built to do, and that is where each story begins.
