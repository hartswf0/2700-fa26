TITLE
The Cyberiad, Part One: ten robot stories by Stanisław Lem, drawn in ink

DESCRIPTION
Part one of The Cyberiad, filmed: ten short films after Stanisław Lem, drawn in ink, about an hour long. Two constructors, Trurl and Klapaucius, build machines for kings and for each other, and every machine does exactly what it was built to do.

In this hour:
A machine that can make anything beginning with N is asked to make Nothing.
An eight-story computer insists that two and two make seven.
A wish machine makes a second Trurl, and Klapaucius takes him down to the cellar.
Two armies are wired into two minds that would rather think than fight.
A poetry machine writes to order, and the poets of the land cannot keep up.
A king who has hunted everything orders a beast clever enough to beat him.
Dragons get loose as soon as someone raises their probability.
A prince sick with love is hooked to a machine with every dial at maximum.
A king who loves hide-and-seek gets hold of a device that moves minds between bodies.
Something lands on a planet, survives every weapon, and is finally beaten with paperwork.
