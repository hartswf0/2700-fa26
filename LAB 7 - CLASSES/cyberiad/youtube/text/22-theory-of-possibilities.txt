TITLE
The Theory of Possibilities | an afterword to Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
An essay film after Ewelina Woźniak-Czech: literary theory as a field of possibilities that exist side by side, and Lem’s books that did not exist, now being written by machines.

The bitic study with a stage added: concepts with name-card heads turn, fall and travel, a gapped text keeps several readings at once, and the shelf changes in every scene, from theories to blank-spined books that do not exist to books written by machines.

Film 22 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
All the films: [playlist link]

In the browser: https://hartswf0.github.io/2700-fa26/LAB%207%20-%20CLASSES/cyberiad/

The programming idea: Keeping possibilities: a class that holds every reading of a gapped text and switches between them, compared with one that overwrites each reading with the next.
