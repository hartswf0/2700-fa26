TITLE
The Cyberiad, filmed: Stanisław Lem’s robot stories in ink (complete, 2 hours)

DESCRIPTION
Two constructors, Trurl and Klapaucius, build machines for kings and for each other. Every machine does exactly what it was built to do, and that is where each story begins.

Twenty-two short films after Stanisław Lem, drawn in ink and played one after another: The Cyberiad in the order of the book, Prince Ferrix and the Princess Crystal, Non Serviam from A Perfect Vacuum, and five films from A History of Bitic Literature. An afterword reads them through an essay on the theory of possibilities.
