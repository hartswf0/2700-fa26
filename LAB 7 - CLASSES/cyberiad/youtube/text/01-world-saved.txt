TITLE
How the World Was Saved | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Trurl builds a machine that can make anything beginning with N. Klapaucius tests it by asking it to do Nothing, and it starts taking the world away.

Made by subtraction. Each thing the machine removes leaves a starred hole in the picture that stays to the end and eats the title.

Film 1 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
All the films: [playlist link]
The complete cut, 2 hours: [complete cut link]
In the browser: https://hartswf0.github.io/2700-fa26/LAB%207%20-%20CLASSES/cyberiad/

The programming idea: Arrays: push and splice. A second array of holes that is never emptied, so a removal leaves a mark.

Credits
After Stanisław Lem, The Cyberiad (1965), translated by Michael Kandel (1974).
Narration and dialogue paraphrase the source. Nothing is quoted.
Made by Watson Hartsoe with Claude for Computational Media (2700), Georgia Tech, Fall 2026.
Drawn in the browser with JavaScript. The voices are synthetic, made with Kokoro. The sound is made in code.
