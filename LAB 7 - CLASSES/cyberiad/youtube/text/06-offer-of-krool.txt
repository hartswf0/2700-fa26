TITLE
The Second Sally: The Offer of King Krool | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
King Krool has hunted every beast in the galaxy and wants one clever enough to outthink him. Trurl and Klapaucius have twelve days to build it, and the beast they build has demands of its own.

The beast is never drawn as one creature with organs. It is a few hundred small weedy marks that move as one: a low hill of dry grass with eyes.

Film 6 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Distributed control. There is no single part that holds the brain, so there is nothing to smash; and a program keeps the authority you gave it, even when its authors ask for the King back.
