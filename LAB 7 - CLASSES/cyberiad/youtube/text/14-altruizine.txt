TITLE
Altruizine | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
A hermit named Bonhomius tries to make everyone happy with a substance that makes every creature feel what its neighbours feel. It does not go as he planned.

A grid of panels. A feeling starts in one panel and is copied outward into its neighbours, picture printed over picture.

Film 14 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
All the films: [playlist link]

In the browser: https://hartswf0.github.io/2700-fa26/LAB%207%20-%20CLASSES/cyberiad/

The programming idea: A 2D array where each cell updates from its neighbours, and a feedback loop that runs away when the gain is above 1.
