TITLE
The Third Sally: The Dragons of Probability | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Dragons are not impossible, only improbable. Trurl builds a machine that raises the probability, and soon there is money both in dragons and in getting rid of them.

Each dragon is drawn as dots, and a dot appears only when the dragon’s probability passes that dot’s fixed threshold.

Film 7 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Randomness: a threshold stored once in setup() makes a shape fill in; random() called every frame makes it flicker.
