TITLE
A History of Bitic Literature: Theobitistics | Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
In the fifth volume the machines turn to God: mystical writing measured by a word counter, a frozen computer that never finishes its line, and the idea that we are leftovers from the last time the universe was thinking.

A fifth volume joins the shelf. It opens to mystical writing passed through a word counter, then a sine wave drawn between two infinities, a frost-covered machine that never finishes its line, and a scatter of grains that becomes a galaxy.

Film 21 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Measuring information: score each word’s surprise as minus the log of its frequency, and compare two texts.
