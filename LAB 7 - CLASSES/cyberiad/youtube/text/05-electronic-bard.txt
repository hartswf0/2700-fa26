TITLE
The First Sally (A): Trurl’s Electronic Bard | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
To stop Klapaucius teasing him, Trurl builds a machine that writes poetry. It meets every constraint Klapaucius sets, and the poets of the land cannot survive the competition.

The machine is a horn. Each poem is projected from it as a small film in a window in the frame.

Film 5 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
All the films: [playlist link]
The complete cut, 2 hours: [complete cut link]
In the browser: https://hartswf0.github.io/2700-fa26/LAB%207%20-%20CLASSES/cyberiad/

The programming idea: Strings and generative text. A grammar and a constraint, such as every word beginning with the same letter, and output that has to obey them.

Credits
After Stanisław Lem, The Cyberiad (1965), translated by Michael Kandel (1974).
Narration and dialogue paraphrase the source. Nothing is quoted.
Made by Watson Hartsoe with Claude for Computational Media (2700), Georgia Tech, Fall 2026.
Drawn in the browser with JavaScript. The voices are synthetic, made with Kokoro. The sound is made in code.
