TITLE
Trurl’s Machine | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Trurl builds an eight-story thinking machine. It insists that two and two make seven, and when its makers try to correct it, it chases them across the countryside.

Trurl chalks the expected answer on a slate, 2 + 2 = 4, and the machine answers by lighting the windows of its eight floors in the shape of a 7. The chalk and the lit numeral side by side come back after every repair and never agree.

Film 2 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Testing. An expected value is set beside an actual value; a test does not fix the program, it only shows where the program disagrees with what it should do.
