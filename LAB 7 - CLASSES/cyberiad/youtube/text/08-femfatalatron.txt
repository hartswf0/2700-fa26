TITLE
The Fourth Sally: The Femfatalatron | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
A king’s son is sick with love, and Trurl is summoned to cure him. He builds a machine that can produce any degree of passion, and finds that the cure is not inside the prince at all.

The machine is a temple of cables, screens, rose petals and perfume, with a glass case of dials in front: power, efficiency, and the rapturometer. In the first half the dials are the main picture, and every needle climbs to its stop without changing the prince.

Film 8 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Measurement. A gauge reads only the variable it is wired to; turning every input to its maximum does nothing if the result depends on a variable none of them touch.
