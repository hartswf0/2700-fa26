TITLE
The Sixth Sally: The Demon of the Second Kind | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
A pirate with a doctorate captures the constructors and demands information. They build him a demon that pulls true facts out of the air, and give him all of them.

The film is printed on one long paper tape that slides from scene to scene. At the end it is the tape coming out of the Demon’s barrel and burying Pugg.

Film 11 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
