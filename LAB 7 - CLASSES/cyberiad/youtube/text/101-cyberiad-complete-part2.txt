TITLE
The Cyberiad, Part Two: twelve more films after Stanisław Lem, drawn in ink

DESCRIPTION
Part two of The Cyberiad, filmed: twelve short films after Stanisław Lem, drawn in ink, about an hour long. The last two sallies of Trurl and Klapaucius, three tales, two of Lem’s books about books that do not exist, and an afterword.

In this hour:
A pirate with a doctorate demands information and is given every true fact there is.
A deposed tyrant gets a kingdom in a box, with ten thousand subjects who can think.
Three storytelling machines tell stories inside stories, eleven deep, in one shot.
A hermit tries to make everyone feel what their neighbours feel.
A robot prince disguises himself as a human to marry a princess who will have nothing else.
People living inside a computer argue about what, if anything, they owe their maker.
Five films from a history of books written by machines, for readers who are not us.
An afterword on Lem’s books that did not exist, which machines are now writing.
