TITLE
A Good Shellacking | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Trurl sends Klapaucius a wish machine as a present. Klapaucius wishes for a Trurl so exact that no one could tell the two apart, and the machine makes one.

Every Trurl in the film is drawn by the same routine and speaks with the same voice, so the picture never settles which Trurl was in the cellar. The wish machine hands each item out of a door in its belly.

Film 3 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Equality against identity. Two objects can be equal in every field and still be two objects, and nothing you can read from the outside says which one is the original.
