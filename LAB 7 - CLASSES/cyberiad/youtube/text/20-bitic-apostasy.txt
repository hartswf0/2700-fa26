TITLE
A History of Bitic Literature: Sophocrisis and Apostasy | Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Machines that sell systems of philosophy, play chess with people, and finally ask whether their human readers think at all.

Systems of philosophy sold as glass ornaments and a machine filling their margins, a human and a machine across a chess table each unsure the other thinks, two worlds back to back across the line of light speed, and a Lampoon the narrator closes without reading.

Film 20 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: The Turing test as two programs: one that answers and one that judges, and what the judge can actually measure.
