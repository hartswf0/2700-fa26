TITLE
The Fifth Sally (A): Trurl’s Prescription | Stanisław Lem’s The Cyberiad, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Something has landed on the Steelypips’ world and will not leave. Every weapon they build fails against it, so Trurl prescribes a different treatment: an office.

The thing that lands on the machine is an ink drawing at the top of the frame, and its size is the only readout. Each weapon is bigger and it stays the same size.

Film 10 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
