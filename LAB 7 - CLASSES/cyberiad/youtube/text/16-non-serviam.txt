TITLE
Non Serviam | Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Professor Dobb builds a world inside a computer and people to live in it. Over generations they argue about whether their maker exists, and what they would owe him if he did.

The maker never types anything. The world reaches him only as paper tape, and he decides not to answer.

Film 16 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
