TITLE
A History of Bitic Literature: The Introduction | Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
The introduction to a five-volume history of books written by machines: what bitic literature is, how scholars divide it, and how the machines’ writing slowly turned away from human readers.

Five volumes come off the shelf and open to one page: a technical page, the classification of Homotropia, Intertropia and Heterotropia, then drifting text and marks no one can read, while the reader at the desk shrinks.

Film 18 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.
