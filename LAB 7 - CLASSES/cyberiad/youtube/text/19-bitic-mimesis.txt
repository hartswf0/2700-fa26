TITLE
A History of Bitic Literature: Monoetics and Mimesis | Stanisław Lem, filmed

DESCRIPTION
Machines that give old words new meanings, and machines that write the books a dead author left unwritten, including a novel Dostoevsky never wrote.

The same study as the first bitic film. A dictionary whose old meanings are struck through and replaced by machine meanings, a woven many-shelled body grown from a page, a spine titled The Girl dropping into the gap in Dostoevsky’s shelf, and a press stamping out numbered spines.

Film 19 of 22 in The Cyberiad, filmed, a collection of Stanisław Lem’s stories drawn in ink.

The programming idea: Generative text from a corpus: a word-pair generator that continues one author’s text, and why it can only recombine what the author already wrote.
