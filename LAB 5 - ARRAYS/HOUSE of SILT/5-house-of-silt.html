INTERACTIVE: Click anywhere on the water canvas to build a house at that X coordinate.
Hover near any house to read its Knowles quatrain in the top-left HUD.

After Alison Knowles & James Tenney — The House of Dust (1967)

Four discrete arrays: materials, locations, lights, and inhabitants. makeHouse(x) selects one element randomly from each array and returns an object record. houses.push(makeHouse(mouseX)) adds it to the town array, and drawHouse(h) translates each word into visual architectural form.
