    Story & Educational Premise

    The district put attendance on the old TV in Room 4. It marks Finley Current with a C every morning, even the one morning he is early. Ms. Rainey turns the TV around and the class reads the program: a bubble that won't move until someone calls it, a Lost & Found drawer that starts at slot zero, and a crosswalk on Main Street painted by a loop. The bug in the attendance loop is one number.
