THE ANSCOMBE GAME
G.E.M. ANSCOMBE (1957) · INTENTION §32

The Shopping List vs. The Detective

WORDS → WORLD (INTENTION)

The Shopping List

A man goes to the supermarket with a shopping list. If he puts margarine in the trolley instead of butter, the mistake is in his action (the world).

He cannot fix the error by crossing out butter on the list and writing margarine. The world must be made to conform to the words.
WORLD → WORDS (INFERENCE)

The Detective's Notebook

A detective follows the man, writing down every item he selects. If the detective writes margarine when the cart holds butter, the mistake is in the notebook (the words).

The detective fixes the error by erasing the word and writing butter. The words must be made to conform to the world.
WHAT THIS HAS TO DO WITH CODE:

The Programmer is the Shopper: writing code to force the world to satisfy a human intention.
The Visitor is the Detective: observing the running screen to deduce what rule was authored.
