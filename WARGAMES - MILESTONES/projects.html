01Project 8

That One Team

2D platformer · rhythm
Notes

Bouncy movement with a darker mood. Rhythm, timing, a "button presser." AI tools mentioned, role not stated.

Possible loop

Each bounce sets up the next input. Press on the beat and the landing holds; miss it and the next landing is thrown off.

Open

What the darker mood is about. What the AI tools do.

02Group 9

Company Builder

Idle clicker
Notes

Grow a paperclip company and gradually lose control of it. Vengeance is mentioned; its place in the story is unclear.

Possible loop

Early clicks are the player's own decisions. Each upgrade hands a decision to the machinery the player paid for, so expanding production means less say in what the company does next.

Open

Who wants vengeance, and on whom. Whether control can be won back.

clickupgradelose control
03Group 10

Lab-Rat-ory

Escape game · four levels
Notes

A lab animal learns its way out. Training, a plot twist, changes in resolution.

Possible loop

The training is the tutorial: the lab teaches the animal a behaviour for its own purposes, and the player then finds how to use that same behaviour to escape. A change in resolution marks a change in what the player can see or understand about the lab.

Open

What the plot twist is. What changes at each of the four levels.

trainescaperesolution
04Group 15

AI Reliance & Battleship

Strategy · title unclear
Notes

The risks of relying on AI. References to Anthropic, Battleship and an "evil" element. Title and exact mechanics unclear.

Possible loop

An AI advises the player where to fire. The hidden board makes its advice hard to check, so the player may follow a confident call, miss, and then decide how much to trust the next one.

Open

The title. What the "evil" element is. Whether the AI is ever wrong on purpose.

advisefiretrust
05Group 16

Happy Place

Interactive TV piece
Notes

The player clicks between channels. Grayscale TV imagery with pink glitches. The premise is tied to AI in everyday life.

Possible loop

Flip channels to investigate. The same pink disturbance turns up across unrelated programmes, and noticing it is the clue that something connects them.

Open

What connects the channels. How the piece ends.

08Group 25

Glitchquest

Genre-mixing · the game lies
Notes

The game is lying. References to Mario, Space Invaders and Among Us, space imagery, and a possible corrupt-government thread.

Possible loop

Each reference teaches an expectation: what a safe jump looks like, how enemies move, whose claims to doubt. The game breaks one, the player follows an instruction, sees it fail, and starts testing what the interface tells them.

Open

Who is lying, the game or someone in it. Whether the government thread stays.

followdoubttest

Sushi Cutting

Precision · title only
Notes

Only the title is recorded.

Possible loop

The player sets each cut's angle, position and timing to make a set of even pieces, then sees them side by side. A first cut slightly off centre leaves less room for the rest, so each slice changes the next.
